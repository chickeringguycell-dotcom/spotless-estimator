SECTION I: DETAILED SCOPE OF JANITORIAL SERVICES
Client: Apex Technology
Service Area Standard Janitorial Cleaning Procedures Frequency
1. Offices, Workstations & Conference Rooms • Empty all desk, office & conference wastebaskets; replace liners.
• Remove designated recyclable paper & clean container bins.
• Wipe and sanitize open, accessible desk surfaces & conference tables.
• Dust accessible horizontal surfaces, credenzas & windowsills.
• Disinfect door handles, light switches, push bars & high-touch points. 		Each Visit
2. Floor Care & Maintenance • Vacuum all carpeted suites, main walkways, aisles & entry floor mats.
• Sweep and dust-mop all hard surface floors with microfiber heads.
• Damp-mop hard surface floors with neutral commercial cleaner.
• Spot-clean normal floor spills, watermarks & scuffs where accessible. 		Each Visit
3. Commercial Restrooms • Clean, descale & disinfect toilet bowls, seats, urinals & hardware.
• Wash and sanitize sinks, faucets, counters & splash areas.
• Clean and polish mirrors and stainless steel/chrome fixtures.
• Empty sanitary receptacles and trash bins; install fresh liners.
• Mop and sanitize restroom floors with hospital-grade disinfectant.
• Restock client-provided hand soap, paper towels, and toilet tissue. 		Each Visit
4. Kitchens & Break Areas • Sanitize dining tables, countertops, food prep areas & backsplashes.
• Wash, disinfect & polish sinks and chrome faucets.
• Wipe exterior surfaces of microwave, refrigerator, coffee station & cabinets.
• Empty all food waste bins and replace heavy-duty garbage liners.
• Sweep and mop breakroom hard floors with degreasing neutral cleaner. 		Each Visit
5. Interior Glass & Partitions • Spot-clean interior glass entrance doors, sidelights & push plates.
• Remove fingerprints & smudges from interior glass conference partitions. 		Each Visit
6. Waste Removal & Recycling • Transport all collected bagged trash & recycling to client's onsite dumpster/chute. Each Visit
Clarified Service Exclusions (Not Included in Standard Janitorial Agreement):
Spotless Office Solutions specializes in recurring commercial office janitorial maintenance. The following specialty services are explicitly excluded from this contract unless a separate written agreement is executed: construction debris cleanup, pressure washing, floor stripping/waxing/buffing/refinishing, machine floor restoration, exterior high-rise window washing requiring climbing equipment, washing employee dishes, cleaning inside ovens/refrigerators, and moving personal items/documents.

SECTION II: COMMERCIAL SERVICE TERMS & CONDITIONS
Client: Apex Technology

1. Service Schedule & Operating Hours: Spotless Office Solutions ("Company") will perform agreed janitorial services according to the contracted frequency on designated service days during agreed after-hours windows.

2. Facility Access & Security: Client agrees to provide Company personnel with necessary building keys, electronic access fobs, and security alarm arm/disarm codes. Company maintains strict key control protocols and background-screened staff.

3. Customer Utilities & Responsibilities: Client will provide continuous access to electrical outlets, running hot/cold water, custodial utility sinks, and designated onsite waste dumpsters/recycling bins at no charge to Company.

4. Supplies & Equipment: Company furnishes all commercial cleaning equipment (HEPA vacuums, mops, microfiber systems) and professional EPA-registered disinfectants. Client supplies consumable paper products, trash liners, and hand soaps unless separate consumable supply service is ordered.

5. Invoicing & Payment Terms: Invoices are generated electronically on the first (1st) business day of each service month. Payment terms are Net 30 Days from invoice date. Payments may be remitted via ACH, corporate check, or approved electronic transfer.

6. Holidays & Schedule Changes: Recognized legal holidays (New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas) falling on scheduled service days will be serviced on the prior or following business day by mutual coordination.

7. Quality Guarantee & Corrections: Company maintains a 24-Hour Quality Correction Guarantee. Any service discrepancy reported within 24 hours will be inspected and corrected at no additional charge on or before the next scheduled visit.

8. Agreement Term & Cancellation: This Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial term of twelve (12) months from the Commencement Date and shall renew automatically on a month-to-month basis. Either party may terminate this Agreement without penalty upon delivering thirty (30) days advance written notice.

9. Property Damage & Insurance: Company carries comprehensive commercial general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage. Any accidental damage to client property must be reported to Company management within 48 hours for immediate claim handling.

10. Safety & Hazardous Conditions: Company personnel are not permitted to handle biohazards, chemical spills, needles, mold remediation, or unsafe working heights. Client shall maintain a safe premises conforming to OSHA standards.

Legal Notice: These commercial terms represent standard janitorial operating guidelines. Significant liability limitations, indemnification provisions, and dispute resolution terms remain subject to formal legal review per corporate counsel requirements.
